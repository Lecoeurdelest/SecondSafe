# Flow review

AC-MOD-06-2. Inspected `resolveDispute`, `refundDisputedOrder`, `releaseDisputedOrder`, `completeReleasedOrder` and `increaseViolationAndMaybeSuspend` in `backend/src/modules/moderator/moderator.service.js`. A resolved dispute is refused first, then a dispute that is not under investigation, then a resolution other than `refund` or `release`, then a missing escrow hold; nothing has changed at that point. Refund calls `escrowService.refundFunds` when the hold is `held` and refuses when it was already released. Release calls `escrowService.releaseFunds` when the hold is `held`, refuses when it was already refunded, then completes the order and marks the listing sold. Only after the money step succeeds is the losing party's violation recorded and the dispute saved as `resolved`, so a failed settlement leaves the dispute open. The escrow service itself refuses a hold that is not `held`, which makes a repeated settlement harmless.
